\section*{Purpose}
Quantify what, in the certified Liang-1997 reproduction, is \emph{reproducible} across independent re-runs of the frozen solver, and what is not. No code, tolerance, or mesh was changed. The certified reference is the 2026-09-24 cold run (pure-Python kernel): $G_{ii}=3.075619/2.136180/2.387429\ten{25}$ at $71\times58/100\times70/141\times85$; non-monotone, $S_{\mathrm{rev}}=737$, Richardson suppressed.

\section*{Why absolute $G_{ii}$ is not reproducible ($\sim$2--3\%)}
Two mechanisms, both amplified by $G_{ii}$ being a near-null-space, hotspot-sensitive moment:

\paragraph{(1) Plateau-gate false-pass on a transient flat spot.} The gate certifies when the last-4 cumulative-iteration spread of $G_{ii,\max}$ falls below $3\ten{-4}$. A locally flat window \emph{mid-transient} can trip it early. The Windows \emph{clean, pure-Python} 100$\times$70 did exactly this:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
\toprule
 & chunk 3 & chunk 4 & \dots & baseline chunk 7 \\
\midrule
cum iters & 240 & 300 & & 480 \\
$G_{ii}$ ($\ten{25}$) & 1.949 & \textbf{2.0876} & & 2.1362 \\
last-4 spread & $1.1\ten{-2}$ & \textbf{$1.2\ten{-4}$ PASS} & & $1.9\ten{-4}$ PASS \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
$G_{ii}$ had just risen $+7\%$ (chunk 3$\to$4) --- clearly still climbing toward $\sim$2.136 --- yet the last-4 window was momentarily flat, so the gate certified $2.0876\ten{25}$ ($-2.3\%$) at 300 iters instead of 480. This affects \emph{clean, same-kernel} runs; it is not a memory/OOM/interruption artifact.

\paragraph{(2) numba vs.\ pure-Python kernel divergence.} The Linux box ran the numba JIT assembly kernel (\texttt{SHE2D\_DISABLE\_NUMBA} unset). Though numba ``changes only how the same discrete equations are assembled'' (FP rounding/order), the 71$\times$58 trajectory diverged from the reference \emph{at chunk 0} (iters 1--60: $2.6433$ vs reference $2.5571\ten{25}$) and converged to $2.9847\ten{25}$ ($-2.9\%$). Its 100$\times$70 entered a hotspot limit-cycle ($\sim$1.80$\ten{25}$, spread stuck at $2$--$4\ten{-3}$) that the pure-Python kernel did \emph{not} exhibit. Thus $\sim$$10^{-15}$ kernel rounding differences amplify to $\sim$3\% in the converged $G_{ii}$.

\section*{Conclusion}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Reproducible:} the non-monotone trio $\Rightarrow$ Richardson suppressed $\Rightarrow$ no justified continuum $G_{ii}$. Confirmed in every run; the YELLOW / non-asymptotic verdict stands and is \emph{strengthened}.
\item \textbf{Not reproducible:} the absolute $G_{ii}$ of the two larger nodes, to better than $\sim$2--3\%, owing to gate false-passes, kernel differences, and path/warm-start sensitivity of a near-null-space quantity. A clean pure-Python 71$\times$58 \emph{is} bit-reproducible, so the solver itself is deterministic per kernel; the irreproducibility is in where/whether the ill-conditioned plateau is certified.
\item \textbf{Recommended gate-hardening (future, frozen-ref change --- not applied):} require flatness sustained over multiple chunks, or a small chunk-to-chunk $\Delta G_{ii}$, so the gate cannot certify mid-transient. This would make absolute $G_{ii}$ reproducible without affecting the (already robust) qualitative verdict.
\item \textbf{Pending:} Linux pure-Python run (\texttt{SHE2D\_DISABLE\_NUMBA=1}) --- the apples-to-apples clean reproduction; this report will be finalized when it lands.
\end{itemize}
